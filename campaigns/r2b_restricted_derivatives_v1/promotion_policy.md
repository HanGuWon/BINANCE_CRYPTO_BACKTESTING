# R2B promotion policy

`R2B_BLOCKED_IMPLEMENTATION` is the current disposition. Promotion to
`R2B_READY_FOR_PREOUTCOME_FREEZE` requires a reviewed signal-semantics
amendment for both premium features, an executable 36-row registry, explicit
directional tests, a slow-reference qualification over all three timeframes
and both UM sides, a clean implementation commit, and a fresh full pytest
receipt. No performance result or holdout access is a prerequisite or may be
substituted for these gates.
