# R2B aggregation erratum 001 — v6 aggregator superseded

The historical `aggregate_1.json` and `aggregate_2.json` retained under the
immutable v6 checkpoint root are preserved as superseded evidence. Their
implementation used trade-row means for fold/HAC inference, monthly trade-level
collapse for bootstrap, and did not emit the frozen concentration,
positive-fold, catastrophic-reversal, or temporal-replication gates.

Corrected R2B aggregation uses the pre-outcome R2A2 contract: equal-weight
decision-time portfolio observations, calendar-month blocks sampled jointly
across symbols (1,000 draws, seed 1729), full-family BH-FDR q=0.05, positive
fold fraction defined as `mean(fold_aggregate_mean > 0)`, aggregate HAC,
aggregate absolute top-symbol share, and
`worst_fold_mean < -2 * best_fold_mean` catastrophic reversal. All gates are
required for `TEMPORAL_REPLICATION`; otherwise the result is `NO_REPLICATION`
or `INSUFFICIENT_FOLDS`.

The corrected artifacts are generated by `scripts/aggregate_r2b.py` from the
sealed v7 root and are repeated from the same root to prove deterministic
hashes. No hypothesis, checkpoint, or holdout boundary is changed here.
