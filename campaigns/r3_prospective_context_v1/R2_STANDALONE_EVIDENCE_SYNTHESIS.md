# Standalone historical evidence synthesis

This is a negative-evidence summary, not a new hypothesis-selection exercise.
No trial is ranked, promoted, reversed, or selected by observed performance.

## R2A.2

The preregistered standalone campaign contained 756 temporal/horizon trials.
Under the frozen costs and temporal-replication criteria, temporal replication
was 0. The historical final holdout remains `UNTOUCHED`.

## Corrected R2B

The corrected premium campaign contained 72 preregistered trials. Temporal
replication was 0, and the historical final holdout remains `UNTOUCHED`.

## Scientific implication

Standalone historical indicator evidence has not produced a robust promotion
candidate under the frozen costs and temporal-replication criteria. This
motivates a new, explicitly prospective question: can indicators provide
useful information as causal context, gates, or state variables when combined
with simple causal price signals? This R3 question is not a retrospective
re-analysis of the observed R2 outcomes.
