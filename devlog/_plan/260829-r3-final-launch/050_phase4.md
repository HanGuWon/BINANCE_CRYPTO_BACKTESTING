# R3 p4 — engineering shadow gate

The full Top50 shadow is deliberately **not run**. The canonical August roster
is reproducible, but no completed August ranking is available to derive the
September roster. Starting a full shadow with an invented or stale membership
would violate the causal universe contract. `R3_ENGINEERING_SHADOW_BLOCKED.json`
records the refusal with zero symbols collected and no outcome/holdout access.
